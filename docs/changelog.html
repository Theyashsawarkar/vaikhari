v0.4.12026-11-04release ↗

Fixes from an independent review of 0.4.0, each with a test:

  • A post can't go out twice from Retry: two retries at once (a double click, or the screen and the page) posted to LinkedIn twice. A retry now claims the post first.
  • No key answers an approval popup as it opens: Ctrl+S and Enter could, if you were typing elsewhere when it appeared. Now every key waits a moment.
  • An agent's note can't repaint the screen: escape sequences in it, or in a post's text in the lists, are dropped.
  • The browser screen keeps what you see: a scheduled post whose change is refused stays scheduled (it became a draft while the page said "Scheduled"); Post now saves what you typed last and keeps it if nothing went out; an agent's post can't get images it didn't have; leaving edits to a scheduled or failed post asks first.
  • Approving a post with a refused edit changes nothing (it used to keep the networks or time you'd picked).
  • Opening a post from a list keeps what you were writing, as a draft.
  • The LinkedIn secret is always private: config.toml is written 0600, even when you made it yourself first.
  • A due X post notifies while vaikhari web runs, as it does otherwise.
  • The browser's settings don't overwrite newer ones, and a new LinkedIn app there ends the old sign-in.
  • Images: two screens in one kitty window no longer share image ids, and no temporary picture is left behind.
  • Settings: Tab while editing a field goes to the next field.
  • X: numbered thread parts always fit in 280; only real x.com and twitter.com links count as a post's link.

v0.4.02026-11-03release ↗

  • A logo and a shloka at the top of the README and the website: every Vayu tool has them now.
  • vaikhari web: the same posts in your browser. Everything the screen does, with previews that look like X's and LinkedIn's own: writing, threads, images (pick, paste or drop them), the schedule, the Queue, approvals, X's one click, settings and LinkedIn's sign-in. It listens on 127.0.0.1 only and every call needs a token that only its page holds. Works on a phone-sized window too.
  • Settings tab: set up LinkedIn inside the screen, with the steps beside the fields, and your name and handle for X's preview. Signing in stays in the screen too, without dropping to a terminal prompt. It's read-only until you press Enter on a field, and changing the app of a working sign-in asks first.
  • Tab no longer gets stuck in the editor: Tab and Shift+Tab switch tabs while you write, and what you wrote stays.
  • Signing in again asks first when your sign-in is still good, so a stray key opens no browser tab.
  • Capsules on the approval screen: Tab and Shift+Tab switch the frame between the post, each network's preview and each image (these replace p); o opens the image full size.
  • Images you can see: previews draw a post's image where it will sit. In kitty it's the real image, even inside a tmux popup; other terminals get colour half-blocks. WebP images aren't drawn: o opens them.
  • X says what happened to your image: its window says the image is on your clipboard, or why it couldn't be copied, and Ctrl+Y copies it again.
  • A post of nothing but --- lines now counts as empty, everywhere.
  • ApproveAs takes a time below 0 to mean "now".

v0.3.02026-11-03release ↗

  • The approval screen as designed, the same layout as narada's and sangam's: networks, place in the queue and "nothing posts until you answer" on top; one sentence, what you asked and what's at stake; each network's facts (who it posts as, its length against the limit, where LinkedIn folds it, X's thread, how it goes out, the image) beside the post exactly as it goes, with LinkedIn's fold and X's thread breaks marked; the answers as a row of keys. Narrow, the facts stack above the post.
  • New answers: 1 / 2 approve a post for LinkedIn or X only, t approves it for another time, p previews it as each network shows it.
  • After you answer, the popup says what happened (approved, posted where, what still waits for your click, or declined) before it closes.

v0.2.22026-11-03release ↗

  • The approval popup has the same frame as narada's and sangam's: a red rounded border titled "vaikhari · approve a post", red because a post is public.

v0.2.12026-11-02release ↗

  • A new approval screen: one sentence says who wants to post, publicly and as you, on which networks; then what you asked, and labels for what's at stake. The post sits beside each network's preview, and the answer says what it does ("Post on both").
  • A key pressed as the popup opens (you were typing elsewhere) can't answer it.

v0.2.02026-11-02release ↗

  • Renamed from prasar to vaikhari (वैखरी, speech spoken aloud: the last of the four stages of speech, the one the world hears). The command is vaikhari, the repository github.com/Theyashsawarkar/vaikhari (the old address redirects), the MCP server vaikhari. The first run moves your folders, database and LinkedIn sign-in to the new name; kosha update moves the install.
  • A clearer approval: a post waiting for you reads as one sentence ("Claude Code wants to post to X and LinkedIn"), marked public and can't be unsent, with the agent's reason quoted and the post shown as X and LinkedIn will show it, side by side.
  • Screenshots in the README and on the website.

v0.1.12026-10-28release ↗

  • The previews share their column by what they hold: a long X thread gets the room it needs (up to two thirds), so its last part is no longer cut off while LinkedIn's box stood half empty.

v0.1.02026-10-28release ↗

  • First release: write once, post to X and LinkedIn from the terminal.
  • The screen: an editor with live previews of the post as X shows it (X's weighted count, threads split at sentence ends or at --- lines, numbered) and as LinkedIn's feed shows it (folded at "…see more"), each with a meter; tabs for Compose, Queue, Drafts and Posted; a key sheet (?); your varna theme's colours, live. Closing it keeps what you wrote as a draft.
  • LinkedIn through its free API with an app of your own (prasar auth linkedin --setup walks through it): text with real hashtags, images, the 60-day sign-in counted down in the corner.
  • X with one click, free: X's share page opens with the post filled in, you press Post and confirm it (pasting its link makes a thread's next part a reply); images go to the clipboard to paste.
  • Drafts and a schedule (tomorrow 9am, fri 6pm, in 2h...), with prasar service install publishing scheduled posts in the background; a post is claimed before it's sent, so it never goes out twice.
  • For agents: prasar mcp (post_check, post_draft, post_publish, post_list, post_accounts); every post an agent proposes waits for your approval in a tmux popup (prasar approve), where you can edit it.
  • CLI: prasar post, check, list, approve, auth, service, daemon, doctor, demo (a fake LinkedIn, nothing posted).
  • A TPM plugin (prefix B), an installer, release binaries, and a website.
